Privacy policy

We only collect what the market needs to work, and we do not sell or share your details. This page explains what we collect, why, and how to ask us to remove it.

Last updated: November 25, 2026. Locally Crafted CA is a small community market in Canada. If you have a question about your details, write to us at contact@locallycrafted.ca.

What we collect

From makers who submit a product: your name, your business name, your email, the product name, its type (and your own words if you pick “Other”), a short description, its price, and one photo of the product.

From the contact form: your name, your email, what you make, and your message.

From buyers who reserve a piece: your name, email, phone number, how many you want, whether you want pickup or delivery, any message you add, and which piece you are reserving — so the maker can get in touch with you.

Visits: we keep a simple count of how many times the site is visited, so we can see whether the market is growing. The count is a number only — it does not identify you.

Why we collect it

Only to run the market: to review products before they go live, to reply to makers and buyers, and to pass reservations on to the maker. We do not use your details for anything else, and we do not sell them or give them to advertisers.

What goes public

Only what you see on a listing: the product, its price, its photo, its description, and the maker’s business name. Your email address is never shown on the site, and nothing you submit appears on the site until we have reviewed and approved it.

How it is stored, and who sees it

Submissions, reservations and messages are kept in the market’s database and in the operator’s email inbox. Photos are kept in the market’s file storage. Only the market operator sees submissions and reservations before they are approved — makers do not have accounts or logins on this site, and we do not store passwords. Contact-form messages are sent to the operator's email.

We do not use advertising trackers. The site keeps a couple of small settings in your own browser only — for example, the market team can switch off visit-counting for their own device from Manage (“Do not count my visits”).

How long we keep it

We keep a listing’s details while the listing is live, and for a short time after, so we can answer questions about it. Messages and reservations are kept while they are needed to follow up, then removed.

Asking for your details to be removed

If you would like your listing, your details, or a message removed, write to contact@locallycrafted.ca and tell us what to take down. We will remove it and let you know when it is done.

If this policy changes

If we start collecting something new, or change how we use your details, we will update this page and note the date at the top.
