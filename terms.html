Terms of use

A short, plain-English guide to how the market works — for makers listing their work, and for neighbours reserving a piece.

Last updated: November 25, 2026. The market is run by Locally Crafted CA. Questions? Write to contact@locallycrafted.ca.

What the market is

Locally Crafted CA is a community boutique where local makers list handmade pieces. Buyers browse the site and reserve a piece they like. There is no checkout on the site — the maker contacts the buyer, and they arrange pickup, delivery and payment between themselves. Makers keep 100% of their sales. We charge no commission.

Listing a product

Makers send in a product through the “Submit a product” form — one product per form, with one photo. Every submission is reviewed by us before anything goes live. We may refuse a listing — for example, if it is not handmade, if the details are incomplete or misleading, or if it does not fit the market. A refused listing simply never appears on the site.

Packages and prices

December 2026 is free for makers — list up to 10 products. From January 2027, the packages are:

  • Silver — 10 listings, $19.99/month.
  • Gold — 25 listings, $29.99/month.
  • Platinum — 50 listings, $39.99/month.

If package prices ever change, makers will be told before the change applies to them.

What makers are responsible for

Makers are responsible for the accuracy of their prices, descriptions and photos, for listing only work they made themselves, and for fulfilling reservations — replying to buyers and arranging pickup or delivery as agreed. If a piece is no longer available, the maker should let us know so the listing can be taken down.

Reservations and payment

A reservation is not a purchase. It tells the maker you would like the piece, and the maker will contact you to confirm. The sale itself — including payment, pickup or delivery, and any returns — is between the buyer and the maker. Buyers pay makers directly, by cash, e-transfer or tap, as the maker arranges. The market does not take payments and is not a party to the sale.

Photos

One photo per product, and it should show the actual piece for sale. Photos must be the maker’s own — please do not send in pictures that belong to someone else.

Listings we can change or remove

We may edit a listing to fix an obvious mistake, or remove a listing at any time — for example, if it was sold, if the details are wrong, or if it no longer fits the market. We will let the maker know when we do.

If these terms change

We may update these terms as the market grows. The date at the top will show when they were last updated, and makers with live listings will be told about changes that affect them.
