Yasmine's Local Market

A friendly little market run by Yasmine, where local makers share the beautiful things they make by hand — pottery, knitting, candles, jewelry, honey and more. There’s no checkout and no card payment on this site. You reserve what you love, and the maker contacts you directly to arrange everything, person to person.

How it works

Reserving something is simple — there is no checkout and no card payment on this site. You reserve, and the maker contacts you directly.

1 Browse the makers’ pieces

Look through the pieces below and find a favourite. Every item shows who made it and the price.

2 Click “Reserve this item”

Fill in a short form with your email, an optional phone number, and a note about what you’d like.

3 The maker replies to you

The maker replies by email to confirm availability and arrange the meeting and payment with you.

More from our makers

Click any item to reserve it — the form is already filled in with that product.

How the exchange happens

You and the maker agree together — meet at the local market, at the library, or another spot you both like. Simple and in person.

How you pay

Directly to the maker, the way you both prefer: cash when you meet, an e-transfer, or a tap payment on their phone. Nothing is paid through this website.

Why reserve first?

Most pieces are one-of-a-kind or made in tiny batches. Reserving lets the maker confirm it’s still available before you make the trip.
